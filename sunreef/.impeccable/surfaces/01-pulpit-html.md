## Direction contract

THESIS: The shipyard tracker as a sail loft's working system: every hull carries its sail number, and every scan is a fix in time. Refuses the grey SaaS dashboard with one blue accent and a stock pin map.

OWN-WORLD: Cool sailcloth-white ground (#f3f5f6), carbon-laminate charcoal rail and header (#1a2026), hairline seams. Colours with jobs: material = sea blue, mould = tooling-gelcoat orange, alert = signal red, ok = green. Barlow for UI, Barlow Condensed bold for sail-number numerals and counts, JetBrains Mono for printed codes (RW, M-, F-, QR). Square panels, 6px controls.

STORY: Anna sees in 3 seconds where material and moulds are and four things to react to; drills into a batch path or a mould passport. Marek confirms a delivery or closes a stage in seconds on a phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pulpit: charcoal rail left, filters row, schematic site plan as the largest region with zone counts as sail-number figures, "Wymaga reakcji (4)" panel right with "Pokaż historię" links.

FORM: Żaglownia (sail loft), grounded list position 5 of 7, assigned by the roll; seed:2244c6be1809036f (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature move: the hull sail-number badge (condensed numeral plate, e.g. "08") marking each jednostka on every screen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
