# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (desktop web dashboard for managers; phone-width web/mobile app for shop-floor workers)

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS mockups: one HTML file per screen with one shared stylesheet, plus PNG exports (desktop 1440 px, mobile 390 px) for a sales presentation. Same convention as the VetSpot mockups in this repo.

## Users

- **Kierownik produkcji (Anna Zielińska):** opens the web dashboard each morning; needs to see where material and moulds are and what needs her reaction today.
- **Shop-floor workers (stolarnia, laminowanie, tapicernia, lakiernia, montaż wnętrz):** use a phone; confirm deliveries, report losses, scan QR at stage start/end, take stock of moulds. Already log in daily to an internal time-tracking app.
- **Utrzymanie ruchu:** records mould repairs.

The direct audience of the deliverable is Sunreef Yachts' production manager in a short sales presentation.

## Product Purpose

Sunreef Yachts (Gdańsk) builds bespoke sailing and power catamarans; many hulls are in production at once for months. Purchasing and warehouse run in Comarch XL; the record ends at the warehouse issue document (RW). The platform extends visibility past the RW: where material went, how much was used or wasted, whether some was used on another hull; and gives production moulds a digital passport (location, usage history, repairs).

## Operating Context

Shipyard halls and an outdoor mould yard; phones used with gloves, in dust, sometimes outdoors. Managers on desktop in an office.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Phase I (week one, no labels or hardware): loss reports and confirmation that material arrived at a workstation; weekly/monthly report.
- Phase II: QR labels on batches, delivery zones and hulls; QR or RFID on moulds; scans at stage start/end; mobile stocktaking; flow dashboard (site map); mould passports; alerts.
- Location always means "location from the last scan", never live tracking. No GPS.
- Out of scope in the mockups: prices or costs in PLN, AI, chat, GPS tracking.

## Brand Commitments

Internal work tool: clearly more modern and practical than Sunreef's marketing site. All UI copy in Polish. All data is sample data; "today" is 20.11.2026.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. Names, hull numbers, batch codes, RW numbers and counts are synthetic sample data supplied by the user.
